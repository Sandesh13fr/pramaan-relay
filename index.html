Build What Moves India · process prototype

A life certificate should not die between systems.

Pramaan Relay turns the gap between a generated Digital Life Certificate and pension-agency acceptance into a traceable, retryable handoff—with one citizen-visible owner at every step.

The process change: once Aadhaar-based biometric verification has successfully generated a certificate before its due date, downstream delivery failure is treated as an agency exception—not as fresh non-compliance by the pensioner.

              What the mechanism guarantees
              
    Stable receipt ID, payload hash, idempotent retries, distinct generation/fetch/decision states, reason-coded rejection, and an exportable event trail.
    
            
    
            
    
              What it cannot guarantee
              
    Legal pension continuation, agency adoption, correctness of live government records, or a response from any real PDA. Those are explicitly outside this prototype.
    
            
    
          
    
        
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    
        
    
          
    Process, not paint
    
          
    Move the responsibility, not the pensioner.
    
          
    The portal already generates a Digital Life Certificate and makes it available to a Pension Disbursing Agency. Pramaan Relay adds a transaction contract around that handoff.

    Relay process
    
            
    Generate → seal → deliver → acknowledge → decide

    Short answers
    
        
    Questions a judge should ask.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    
          Is this another Jeevan Pramaan UI redesign?
          
    No. The visible interface exists to demonstrate a new backend/process contract: generation, delivery, fetch, and decision are separate states with owners and receipts. The current page design could remain unchanged and still adopt the protocol.
    
        
    
        
    
          Why not simply tell pensioners to generate another certificate?
          
    Regeneration moves the cost of an agency-sync failure back to an elderly citizen and creates duplicate ambiguity. A stable handoff ID lets the system retry safely while preserving the original, on-time proof.
    
        
    
        
    
          Does the generated certificate prove the pension should be paid?
          
    No. It proves that a certificate was generated after biometric authentication. The PDA still owns acceptance. Pramaan Relay preserves that separation and proposes a one-time seven-day administrative hold while an on-time certificate is decided.
    
        
    
        
    
          What stops the proposed hold from becoming an endless loophole?
          
    It starts only after an on-time, verifiable generation event and a T+1 breach; requires an active prior pension, a known agency route, and no death, duplicate, fraud, or contradictory-event flag; opens an escalation at the same moment; lasts at most seven days; cannot renew; and closes on acceptance, reasoned rejection, or expiry. These are prototype assumptions that still require public-authority review.
    
        
    
        
    
          Where does OpenAI fit?
          
    Codex is used meaningfully to research the journey, model the state machine, implement the prototype, generate adversarial scenarios, and test the build. In a production pilot, an OpenAI model could normalize unstructured agency remarks into a candidate reason code and plain-language explanation; deterministic rules and a human decision would still own every state change.
    
        
    
        
    
          Is any real Aadhaar, bank, pension, OTP, or biometric data used?
          
    No. The prototype is deliberately self-contained and uses synthetic data only. It calls no government system and stores nothing.
