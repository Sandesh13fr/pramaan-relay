# Pramaan Relay pricing and use

Pramaan Relay is free: **₹0**.

There is no signup, lead form, paywall, trial, data sale, or premium tier. The prototype runs entirely in the browser and resets on refresh.

## What free includes

- Four guided synthetic scenarios
- Free-play state-machine controls
- Citizen-readable status and action owner
- Synthetic paper receipt preview and mock certificate PDF download
- English interface with a short Hindi status summary
- Mobile, keyboard, and reduced-motion support

## Limits

- It does not submit or retrieve a real Digital Life Certificate.
- It does not connect to Jeevan Pramaan, EPFO, a bank, or any pension authority.
- It does not provide legal advice or guarantee pension continuation.
- The displayed one-time seven-day administrative hold is a policy simulation, not current law or a payment guarantee.
